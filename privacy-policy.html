01. Introduction

Welcome to Wallora. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information you may provide while using our website.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it may be used, and the choices available to you when using Wallora.

02. Information We Collect

Wallora is designed to allow visitors to browse and discover wallpapers without requiring an account for basic use.

Depending on how you interact with the website, certain technical or usage information may be collected automatically.

  • Device and browser information
  • General website usage information
  • IP address and approximate location information where applicable
  • Pages or content that you interact with
  • Information you voluntarily provide when contacting us

03. How We Use Information

Information may be used to operate, maintain and improve the Wallora experience.

  • To provide and maintain our website
  • To understand how visitors use Wallora
  • To improve website performance and design
  • To identify and resolve technical problems
  • To respond to messages and support requests
  • To protect the website against abuse or unauthorized activity

04. Cookies

Wallora or third-party services used on the website may use cookies or similar technologies.

Cookies can help websites remember preferences, understand visitor activity and provide relevant functionality.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Some website functionality may be affected if cookies are disabled.

05. Third-Party Services

Wallora may use third-party services to support website functionality, analytics, advertising, hosting or other technical requirements.

These third-party providers may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

Important: Wallora does not control the privacy practices of third-party websites or services. We recommend reviewing their respective privacy policies when interacting with them.

06. Security

We take reasonable measures to help protect information associated with our website.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

07. Children's Privacy

Wallora does not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

08. Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, technology or legal requirements.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page along with the latest revision date.

09. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, you can contact the Wallora team.
