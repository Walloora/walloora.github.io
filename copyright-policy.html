Copyright Policy

Wallora respects the intellectual property rights of artists, photographers, designers, creators, and copyright owners.

Last Updated: August 2026

This Copyright Policy explains how Wallora handles copyright concerns, intellectual property rights, and copyright infringement reports relating to content available through the platform.

01

Respect for Copyright

Wallora respects the rights of copyright holders and expects all users, contributors, and visitors to respect those rights as well.

Copyright may protect original creative works including photographs, illustrations, artwork, wallpapers, graphics, videos, written material, logos, designs, and other creative works.

Nothing on Wallora should be interpreted as transferring ownership of copyrighted material to Wallora unless explicitly stated.

02

Ownership of Content

Unless otherwise stated, the respective copyright owner retains ownership of their original work.

Wallora does not claim ownership of third-party copyrighted material simply because that material is displayed, referenced, linked, or made available through the platform.

03

Permission to Use Content

Users should only upload, submit, publish, distribute, or make available content when they have the necessary rights, permissions, licenses, or other lawful basis to do so.

  • Use content that you created yourself.
  • Use content that you have permission or a valid license to use.
  • Follow the conditions of applicable licenses, including Creative Commons licenses.
  • Do not remove copyright notices or ownership information from protected works.
04

Copyright Infringement Reports

If you believe that material available through Wallora infringes your copyright, you may contact us with sufficient information for us to identify and evaluate the reported material.

A copyright report should generally include:

  • Your name and contact information.
  • A description of the copyrighted work that you believe has been infringed.
  • The URL or clear location of the material being reported.
  • An explanation of why you believe the use is unauthorized.
  • A statement confirming that the information provided is accurate.
  • Evidence of ownership or authorization to act on behalf of the copyright owner, where appropriate.
05

Review and Removal

After receiving a copyright complaint, Wallora may review the reported material and determine what action, if any, is appropriate.

Depending on the circumstances, Wallora may restrict access to, remove, disable, or request changes to reported material.

A complaint does not automatically establish that copyright infringement has occurred. Wallora may request additional information before taking action.

06

Repeat Infringement

Wallora may restrict, suspend, or terminate access to the platform for users who repeatedly submit, publish, or distribute material that infringes the rights of others.

07

Third-Party Content

Wallora may reference or display links to content hosted by third-party websites or services.

Wallora does not necessarily control third-party content, ownership, licensing, copyright status, or intellectual property practices.

Users should verify the applicable copyright and license terms before downloading, reproducing, modifying, distributing, or commercially using third-party content.

08

False or Abusive Claims

Copyright complaints should be submitted honestly and in good faith.

Knowingly submitting false, misleading, fraudulent, or abusive copyright claims may result in appropriate action.

09

Changes to This Policy

Wallora may update this Copyright Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the platform, applicable requirements, or our practices.

The latest version of this policy will be published on this page.

10

Contact Wallora

For copyright concerns, infringement reports, licensing questions, or other intellectual-property matters, please contact Wallora using the official contact information provided on the website.
