Legal & Copyright

DMCA Policy

Wallora respects the intellectual property rights of creators, photographers, artists, and copyright owners. This page explains how copyright holders can report material that they believe infringes their rights.

Important: Wallora is committed to respecting copyright and intellectual property rights. If you believe that content available through Wallora infringes your copyright, you may contact us with the information described below.

01

Copyright Infringement Notification

If you are a copyright owner or are authorized to act on behalf of a copyright owner, you may submit a copyright infringement notification to Wallora.

Your notification should contain sufficient information for us to identify the copyrighted work and the material that you believe infringes that work.

02

Information Required

A copyright complaint should include the following information:

  • Your full name and contact information.
  • Identification of the copyrighted work that you believe has been infringed.
  • A description of the material you believe infringes your copyright.
  • The URL or other specific location where the allegedly infringing material can be found.
  • A statement explaining why you believe the material infringes your copyright.
  • A statement confirming that the information provided is accurate to the best of your knowledge.
  • Your electronic or physical signature.
03

Review of Complaints

Once a valid copyright complaint is received, Wallora may review the information provided and determine what action is appropriate.

Depending on the circumstances, Wallora may remove or restrict access to the material identified in a valid complaint.

We may contact the person who submitted or uploaded the material when additional information or clarification is necessary.

04

Good-Faith Requirement

Copyright complaints should only be submitted when the complainant genuinely believes that the identified material infringes their copyright or the copyright of someone they are legally authorized to represent.

False, misleading, or fraudulent copyright claims may cause unnecessary harm and should not be submitted.

05

Counter-Notification

If material has been removed or restricted as a result of a copyright complaint and you believe that the removal was made in error, you may contact Wallora and explain the basis for your objection.

Wallora may review the information provided and determine whether the material should be restored, subject to applicable law and the circumstances of the complaint.

06

Repeat Infringement

Wallora may take appropriate action against users who repeatedly upload or distribute material that infringes the intellectual property rights of others.

Such action may include restricting access to content, suspending accounts, or taking other appropriate measures.

07

Third-Party Content

Wallora may contain links, references, or content originating from third parties. Wallora does not claim ownership of copyright-protected material belonging to third parties.

Copyright owners who believe that third-party material displayed or referenced through Wallora infringes their rights may contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

08

Policy Updates

Wallora may update this DMCA Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, applicable laws, or our practices.

Any updated version will be published on this page.
